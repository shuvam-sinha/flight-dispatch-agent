# Emergency descent
category: emergency

An emergency descent is warranted by pressurisation failure, an
uncontrolled fire, or any condition where remaining at altitude is more
dangerous than descending rapidly.

With a pressurisation failure the order is oxygen first. Consciousness is
the prerequisite for everything else, and time of useful consciousness at
high altitude is measured in seconds.

Then descend at the maximum rate the airframe allows: power to idle,
speed brakes as fitted, and a bank to increase the rate while keeping the
speed within limits. In turbulence or with suspected structural damage,
use a lower speed -- a rapid descent that overstresses the airframe has
solved nothing.

Level off at a safe altitude. 10,000 ft is standard where terrain allows,
and the minimum safe altitude for the sector where it does not.

Declare the emergency and squawk 7700.
