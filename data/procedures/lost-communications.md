# Lost communications
category: emergency

First establish that the failure is yours. Check volume, squelch, the
correct frequency, headset connections and the audio panel selection
before assuming the radio has failed.

Squawk 7600. Radar identification continues and controllers will expect
standard procedure.

Under IFR in the United States, route by the last assigned clearance;
then the route ATC advised to expect; then the filed route. Altitude is
the highest of the last assigned, the minimum en route altitude for the
segment, or the altitude ATC advised to expect.

In visual conditions, or on encountering them, continue visually and land
as soon as practicable.

At a towered airport without radio, watch for light signals: steady green
to land, flashing green cleared to approach, steady red give way and
continue circling, flashing red airport unsafe.
