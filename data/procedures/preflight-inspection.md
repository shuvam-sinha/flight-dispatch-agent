# Preflight inspection
category: preflight

Walk the aircraft in a consistent order every time, so a missed item is
obvious rather than forgotten. Check control surfaces move freely and in
the correct sense, hinges and rod ends are secure, and static wicks are
present.

Drain fuel from every sump into a clear tester. Look for water, which
settles clear and heavy at the bottom, and for sediment or a colour that
does not match the grade you expect. Confirm the fuel grade and quantity
visually at the filler -- gauges are the least reliable instrument in a
light aircraft.

Check oil quantity against the minimum for flight, not merely against
"some oil". Confirm the dipstick and filler cap are secure afterwards.

Remove all covers, plugs, chocks and tiedowns, and confirm the pitot
cover is off. A blocked pitot gives an airspeed indication that behaves
like an altimeter, which has killed people who did not expect it.
