# Diversion and alternates
category: planning

An alternate is a plan, not a paperwork entry. Choosing one after the
weather closes in is choosing under pressure with less fuel.

Under FAR 91.169 an IFR alternate is required unless the destination has
an instrument approach and forecast weather from one hour before to one
hour after arrival is at least 2,000 ft ceiling and 3 statute miles
visibility. The airfield chosen must itself meet alternate minimums.

Judge an alternate on more than distance. Approach type and minimums,
runway length and surface, fuel availability, hours of operation and
whether the weather system affecting the destination also affects the
alternate all matter. An alternate 20 miles away under the same front is
not an alternate.

Divert early. The decision costs fuel, and fuel is what makes it
possible.
