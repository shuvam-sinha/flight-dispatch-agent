# Night flight considerations
category: operations

Night flying is legal, routine and less forgiving. Visual references
disappear, terrain is invisible, and an off-airport landing site cannot
be chosen.

Carry a working flashlight and a spare, with red or dim white light to
preserve dark adaptation. Full dark adaptation takes about 30 minutes and
is lost in seconds by a bright light.

Confirm position, anti-collision and instrument lighting all work before
departure, and that the aircraft is legally equipped for night.

Expect illusions. A featureless black approach produces the black-hole
effect, where a normal approach feels high and pilots descend early.
Sloping terrain and runway lighting of unfamiliar intensity both distort
the picture. Fly the instruments and the published profile rather than
what the approach looks like.

Fuel reserves increase to 45 minutes at night under VFR.
