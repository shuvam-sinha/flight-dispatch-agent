# Weight and balance
category: planning

Maximum takeoff weight and centre of gravity limits are both structural
and aerodynamic. Exceeding weight lengthens the takeoff roll, reduces
climb rate and raises stall speed. A centre of gravity outside limits can
make the aircraft uncontrollable in pitch regardless of pilot skill.

Fuel and payload trade against each other. Useful load is maximum
takeoff weight minus empty weight, and it is shared between people,
baggage and fuel. A four-seat trainer with four adults aboard frequently
cannot carry full fuel, and the trap is that nothing about the aircraft
looks wrong on the ramp.

Compute the actual loading for the actual flight. Standard passenger
weights are averages and real passengers are not. Recompute if anything
changes: an extra bag, a fuel top-up, a passenger swap.

Remember that centre of gravity moves as fuel burns off.
