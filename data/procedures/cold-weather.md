# Cold weather operations
category: operations

Contamination on a wing is not acceptable in any quantity. Frost, snow or
ice disrupts airflow enough to prevent a normal takeoff, and the clean
aircraft concept means the surface must be clean, not nearly clean.

Preheat the engine below the manufacturer's stated temperature. Oil that
will not circulate causes damage on start that is not visible until
later.

Carburettor icing is a risk at surprisingly warm temperatures, given
humidity, and can occur at cruise power as well as at idle. Apply carb
heat before reducing power, not after the engine begins to run rough.

Expect longer taxi and warm-up times, and brakes and control surfaces
that may be frozen. Check controls for full and free movement after any
period parked in freezing precipitation.
