# Engine failure in flight
category: emergency

The order is fixed: fly the aircraft, pick a field, then troubleshoot.
Pilots have flown serviceable aircraft into the ground while working a
checklist.

Establish best glide speed immediately and trim for it. Best glide is a
specific number for the aircraft and weight, and both faster and slower
reduce the distance available.

Choose a landing site while altitude still offers a choice, favouring
into-wind, long, smooth and unobstructed over merely close. Commit early;
changing target late costs more than the imperfection of the first
choice.

Then run the restart flow: fuel selector to a tank with fuel, mixture
rich, carburettor heat on, ignition on both, boost pump as fitted.

Declare an emergency. Squawk 7700. Secure fuel and ignition before touch
down, keep the shoulder harness tight, and fly the aircraft all the way
into the crash.
